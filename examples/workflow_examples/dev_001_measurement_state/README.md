## Case classification

- **Workflow operation:** Measurement-state discrimination for intrinsic-property assignment

## Article

- **Title:** A fluorescence turn-off chemosensor based on N-doped carbon quantum dots for detection of Fe³⁺ in aqueous solution
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.matlet.2014.10.168

## Source scope and locations

Main-article page 1, Abstract; page 2, Results and Discussion. Numerical values were taken only from explicit main-text statements that entered the workflow. The fluorescence-decay curve and supplementary files were not used to obtain numerical values.

## Case focus

This case separates the identity of the prepared CDs from their Fe³⁺ measurement state. The same NCDs sample has a baseline lifetime and a lifetime after analyte addition.

## Source-grounded outcome

The final record retains Ex = 370 nm, Em = 415 nm under Ex = 370 nm, and the baseline lifetime of 5.7 ns. The 4.5 ns lifetime after Fe³⁺ addition remains documented in the trace as an analyte-response state.
