# Dev-004: Sample anchoring, controlled condition inheritance, and sample-resolved Em binding

## Case classification

- **Case set:** Representative workflow example
- **Workflow operation:** Sample anchoring, controlled condition inheritance, and sample-resolved Em binding
- **Reviewer comments supported:** R1-C1, R1-C14, R2-C3; supplementary support for R1-C6

## Article

- **Title:** A facile hydrothermal approach towards photoluminescent carbon dots from amino acids
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.jcis.2014.10.030

## Source scope and locations

Main-article page 1, Introduction; page 2, Section 2.2; page 3, Table 1 and its excitation-condition footnotes. Numerical values were taken only from main-article text and textualized main-text Table 1 that entered the workflow. Plotted curves, schematic images, and supplementary files were not used to obtain numerical values.

## Case focus

The explicit CDs–X–T naming rule establishes nine sample anchors from the reported amino-acid and temperature axes. Shared preparation conditions are inherited while precursor identity and temperature remain sample-specific.

## Source-grounded outcome

The final record preserves the nine reported sample identities and their Table 1 Em values. The archived synthesis excerpt documents the corresponding preparation conditions using the same sample identifiers.
