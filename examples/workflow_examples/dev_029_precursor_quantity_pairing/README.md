# Dev-029: Multiple-precursor and quantity pairing

## Case classification

- **Case set:** Representative workflow example
- **Workflow operation:** Multiple-precursor and quantity pairing
- **Reviewer comments supported:** R1-C6

## Article

- **Title:** Cell Nucleus-Targeting Zwitterionic Carbon Dots
- **DOI:** 10.1038/srep18807

## Source scope and locations

Main-article page 2, Results; page 7, Preparation of zwitterionic CDs. Numerical values were taken only from the explicit main-text synthesis sentence and archived extraction and pairing logs that entered the workflow. Plotted figures and supplementary files were not used to obtain numerical values.

## Case focus

This case follows the selected CA:β-Ala 1:2 composition. It illustrates how multiple quantities for one precursor remain associated with that chemical subject while the second precursor retains its own amount.

## Source-grounded outcome

The final record pairs citric acid with 1.0 g and 5.2 mmol, and β-alanine with 10.4 mmol. The archived pairing log and final CSV preserve these quantities without unit conversion or numerical alteration.
