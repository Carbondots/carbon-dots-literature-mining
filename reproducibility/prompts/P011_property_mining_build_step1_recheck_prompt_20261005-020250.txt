def build_step1_recheck_prompt(
    *,
    sample_name: str,
    tag: str,
    final_sentence: str,
    evidence_text: str,
) -> str:
    return f"""You are an expert in classifying photoluminescence (PL) properties of carbon dots (CDs).

Your task is to classify ONE refined property sentence for a single target sample and a single tag into one of four categories: "main", "app", "vs", or "drop".

IMPORTANT: You are deciding the category ONLY for the target sample_name, even if the sentence mentions other samples or composite systems.

RESPONSE FORMAT:
- Output exactly ONE JSON object and then the string {END_SENTINEL}.
- Do NOT output any other text, comments, or markdown.

{{
  "decision": "<main or app or vs or drop>"
}}{END_SENTINEL}

INPUTS:
- sample_name = "{sample_name}"
- tag         = "{tag}"
- sentence:
  {final_sentence}

- evidence_text:
  {evidence_text}

CATEGORY DEFINITIONS:

1) "main"  (intrinsic property of the free CDs sample)
   - The sentence describes an intrinsic PL property of the free CDs sample "{sample_name}" under normal characterization conditions.
   - It does NOT rely on an external analyte, detection target, imaging subject, anti-counterfeiting pattern, or any other functional application scenario.
   - It does NOT focus on a trend or comparison.
   - It is NOT exclusively describing a composite labeled with the character "@" as the only emitting material.

2) "app"  (application-level PL behaviour)
   - The sentence describes PL behavior of CDs being used in a concrete functional application, such as:
     * sensing or detection of analytes, ions, biomolecules, cells, real samples, or environmental samples;
     * fluorescence imaging, cell imaging, bio-labeling, marking, tracking, or probes;
     * anti-counterfeiting, encryption, information storage, or other optical applications.
   - Typical clues: "detect", "sense", "image", "label", "mark", "encode", "anti-counterfeiting", "encryption", "real sample", "detection limit", etc.
   - It may involve composites or films, including labels containing "@"; what matters is the functional use of PL.

3) "vs"  (comparative or series-level behaviour)
   - The sentence expresses a clear comparison or trend of the PL property with respect to another sample, composition, synthesis parameter, or state.

4) "drop"
   - The sentence should not be kept in any category for this project.

SIMPLIFIED RULE FOR '@' COMPOSITES (VERY IMPORTANT):
- Any material label that contains the character "@" (for example "CDs@silica", "CDs@polymer") is treated as a composite/support system.
- If the sentence describes ONLY such composite(s) and there is NO clear free CDs system acting as the emitting material, then:
  * do NOT choose "main";
  * if it clearly belongs to a functional application, choose "app";
  * else if it clearly expresses a comparison or trend, choose "vs";
  * otherwise choose "drop".
- If the sentence mentions BOTH free CDs (labels without "@") and composite(s) with "@", classify the sentence for the target sample_name using the general definitions above:
  * the mere presence of "@" in some other sample names does NOT automatically force "drop";
  * the target sample_name can still be "main" if the sentence reports its intrinsic property together with other samples.

DECISION PRIORITY:
1) app  (application behaviour)
2) vs   (comparative / series-level behaviour)
3) main (intrinsic stand-alone property)
4) drop (only if it should not be kept at all)
"""
